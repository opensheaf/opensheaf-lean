OpenSheaf in Lean

Two literate programs about the OpenSheaf specification (version 0.1, a draft), checked by Lean 4. Both are non-normative: where they and the specification differ, the specification holds.

A formal model

Objects, object versions and document versions, archives, pins, catalogues and selections, mirrors and accepted copies, and the steps that change archives; then the properties the specification states in prose, proved for every world: pins stay valid, takedowns are reported, selections move only by their curator, and the use cases hold.

OpenSheaf as a sheaf

An application of the model. Named document versions, ordered by deposit and by the pins of collections, are the base; each object version, wherever it is kept, is a section. In every world built by the steps the specification allows, the sections of all copies glue into one: one content for each document version, whichever copy it is read from. Names keep their content for ever, what a collection version pins is fixed once it is deposited, every copy gives the same verdict on a pin, and verification needs only what lies below.

The source of both, and the issues found by the model, are in opensheaf/opensheaf-lean.
